# Westinghouse Air Brake Technologies (westinghouse-air-brake-technologies)

Westinghouse Air Brake Technologies Corporation (Wabtec) is a leading global provider of equipment, systems, digital solutions, and value-added services for the freight rail and passenger transit industries. Wabtec's digital platform, WabtecONE, delivers cloud and on-premise solutions for fleet management, condition monitoring, transportation management (RailConnect TMS), performance analytics, predictive maintenance, and signaling.

## APIs

### WabtecONE Platform

Wabtec's digital intelligence platform enabling railroads and industrial operators to connect embedded devices and transmit operational data to the cloud for fleet management, predictive maintenance, and performance analytics.

- **Human URL:** [https://one.wabtec.com/](https://one.wabtec.com/)

### RailConnect Transportation Management System

Wabtec's backbone operational and inventory management system for railroads, providing first mile and last mile connectivity, crew management, and operational automation with API integrations.

- **Human URL:** [https://www.wabteccorp.com/digital-intelligence/transportation-management/railconnect](https://www.wabteccorp.com/digital-intelligence/transportation-management/railconnect)

### Condition Monitoring and Fleet Management

Wabtec's condition monitoring platform (FleetONE and CMMS) providing advanced data management for asset monitoring and predictive maintenance.

- **Human URL:** [https://www.wabteccorp.com/digital-intelligence/condition-monitoring](https://www.wabteccorp.com/digital-intelligence/condition-monitoring)
